# The fake budget

All building and testing happens against a made-up budget on the NUC, never the real one. Built in F0-1.

## What it must have

Enough to exercise every screen, all invented and generated by a seed script (same output every run):

- **Accounts:** checking, savings, two credit cards, a loan, and one account that belongs to "someone else" (to test leaving accounts out of totals). Include an off-budget account if Actual supports it.
- **Categories:** a normal household set in groups (housing, utilities, food, transport, subscriptions, fun, savings goals).
- **History:** 12 months of transactions: paychecks every two weeks, rent or mortgage, utilities, groceries, gas, dining, subscriptions on fixed days, a price increase on one subscription, transfers between accounts.
- **Budgets:** amounts for every month, some categories over, some under.
- **Unusual charges to find:** a duplicate charge, a large one-off at a usual merchant, a new merchant with a big first charge.
- **Schedules:** a few, if Actual's API supports creating them in an import.

## What F0-1 built

Defined in `dev/fake-data.ts` (pure data, seeded random numbers), written by `dev/seed.ts`. The history covers the 12 months ending on the fake budget's "today": the real date, or `FAKE_TODAY=YYYY-MM-DD`. The same "today" always gives the same budget; a test checks this.

- **Budget file:** "Fake Household".
- **Accounts:** Maple Checking, Maple Savings, Comet Visa, Orbit Store Card (on budget); Juniper Auto Loan, Juniper Brokerage (off budget: Actual supports it); Riley's Checking (on budget, "someone else's", with its own paychecks and groceries). Each opens with a starting balance on day 1.
- **Categories:** Housing (Rent, Renters Insurance), Utilities (Electric, Water, Internet, Phone), Food (Groceries, Dining Out), Transport (Gas, Car Insurance, Car Payment), Subscriptions (Streaming, Music, Cloud Storage, Gym), Fun (Hobbies, Entertainment), Savings Goals (Emergency Fund, Vacation, Investing). Income: Salary, Starting Balances.
- **History (about 450 transactions):**
  - Paychecks every other Friday.
  - Rent on the 1st; gym on the 3rd; internet on the 5th; renters insurance on the 10th; electric on the 12th (varies); water on the 18th (varies); car insurance on the 25th.
  - Subscriptions on the store card: StreamFlix on the 7th (goes from $15.49 to $17.99 three months ago), TuneBox on the 14th, CloudVault on the 20th; phone on the Visa on the 22nd.
  - Groceries weekly, gas every 9 days, dining 6 times a month, hobbies and cinema monthly.
  - Transfers: $300 to savings on the 2nd, a $320 loan payment on the 15th, $100 to the brokerage on the 16th; card payments on the 27th for last month's spending.
  - Three notes, to test that notes stay out of logs.
- **Budgets:** the same amounts every month. Dining Out and Hobbies are tight on purpose; groceries go over in the current month (the large charge).
- **Unusual charges** (current month):
  - A duplicate at Noodle Bar (2 × $42.18, same day).
  - $612.40 at FreshMart (usually about $100).
  - A first charge of $899.99 at the new merchant Summit Electronics.
- **Schedules:** Rent, Internet and Car insurance, monthly (`createSchedule` works inside the import).

## Where it runs

An Actual server (`@actual-app/sync-server` 26.11.0, from npm; the NUC has no Docker) on `127.0.0.1:5007`, data in `dev/data/server` (wiped on every start). Its throwaway password and the dev app password are in the git-ignored `dev/.env`, created with random values on first run.

- `npm run dev:fake`: start the server, set its password, seed the budget; Ctrl-C stops it.
- `npm run dev:app`: the app on `127.0.0.1:5180` against it (log in with `DEV_APP_PASSWORD` from `dev/.env`).
