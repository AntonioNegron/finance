# Budget builder: suggestions from his own history, written to Actual only when he confirms

*2026-11-17 · Antonio*

**Decision:**
- Antonio hasn't set up a proper budget yet ("they're always off") and wants help setting it up right.
- The app works out suggested amounts **on the OptiPlex from his own history**, so no one else sees the numbers:
  - bills: the bill amount
  - everyday spending: his typical month (the middle value of recent months)
  - yearly or irregular costs: the yearly total ÷ 12, set aside every month
- He adjusts the suggestions on his phone, then taps confirm, and **the app writes those budget amounts into Actual**. Nothing else is ever written.
- Claude helps in chat with the method and the categories. Advice on his real amounts only by [permission request](../README.md#asking-for-numbers).

**Why:** the least work for him, and his numbers stay home. Phase 2 adds the one write function this needs to [specs/actual-access.md](../specs/actual-access.md), and nothing else.
