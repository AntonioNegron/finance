# Screen changes after the first review, and the app's address

*2026-11-17 · Antonio*

**Decision:**
- **Saving:** savings goals get their own "Saving" section, apart from spending (on Spending and in Home's spent-vs-budget bar).
- **Spending:** categories that are on track fold away under one tap; over-budget and in-progress ones stay visible.
- **Bills:** a "Not a bill" button for false alarms (like the fake Cinema 8), saved in the app only.
- **Address:** `finance.home.lan` for now; it moves with the other apps when the home names (deSEC) are settled.
