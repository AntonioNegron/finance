# A local web app on the OptiPlex: balances, spending vs. budget, bills, unusual charges, then a budget builder

*2026-11-17 · Antonio*

**Decision:**
- **Where:** a web app running locally on the OptiPlex, opened in the phone's browser. No native Android app.
- **What first:** account balances, spending vs. budget by category, upcoming bills and subscriptions, and unusual charges, plus an easier way to build his budget.
- **Changes to Actual:** none until the budget builder is decided ([plan.md](../plan.md) open question 3). Until then the app only reads.

**Why:** he wants something more intuitive than Actual on his phone that shows what he needs to track his finances. He wasn't sure yet whether the app should change anything in Actual, so it starts read-only.
